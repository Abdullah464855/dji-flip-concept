# DJI Flip — Concept Landing Page

Day 3 of a 30-day drone landing page series: one drone, one landing page, every day.

A concept redesign of a product landing page for the **DJI Flip** vlogging camera drone — "The vlog camera that flies itself off your palm." Built as a single self-contained static HTML file (all images inlined, no build step).

## What's on the page
- Light off-white page with a giant "FLIP" wordmark and an enhanced, background-removed hero cutout of the drone
- Spec strip (under 249 g · 48 MP · 4K/60fps HDR · up to 31 min · 13 km FCC)
- "Why Flip stands out" — four numbered cards around a circular front view (foldable full-coverage propeller guards, palm takeoff and landing, AI subject tracking)
- "See Flip in action" gallery panel, three-step "Simple setup, stunning results" cards
- Persona, camera, safety and flight sections; grouped full specifications
- Pick your kit: Flip (RC-N3) from $439 / Flip (RC 2) from $639 / Fly More Combo — price varies by region
- FAQ including the honest notes: obstacle sensing is forward and downward only (the folding cages are the side protection), and obstacle avoidance switches off in AI subject-tracking modes
- Demo checkout pop-up

## Demo checkout
The checkout is a **design demo only** — there is no payment gateway, nothing is sent or stored, and no order is placed. It exists to demonstrate the page's conversion flow.

## Fonts
Open Sans (body/UI) and DM Sans ExtraBold (headlines) — the DJI Store pairing; DJI's own display font (DJI Sans) is proprietary, and DM Sans is the closest free match.
